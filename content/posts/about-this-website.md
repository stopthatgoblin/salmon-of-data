*"It is difficult to make predictions, especially about the future."*

## Why I made this

The purpose of this website is to build a collection of hypotheses about things that may (or may not) happen in future, so that can be tracked over time to see if the things that people expect to see *actually happen*.

As of the time of writing, there is a huge amount of interest, concern and worry about the extent to which AI will impact on the labour market, especially in areas such as coding. By time stamping potential tracks (a high impact, a medium impact and a low impact) I can see how actual data in the future (from the perspective of someone in September 2026) compares.

Unless we've all been converted to paperclips by then, I'll be able to look back from 2030 and see how the real data compares with the possible outcomes.

## How I made this

The website was built with Codex, using the (currently) leading model GPT6-Astra and occassionally earlier models. I don't use AI to write blog posts.
